Post text:
```
Look at the first image. How does it make you feel?

Joyous? Peaceful? Calm? Maybe a bit jealous that you are not in this beautiful setting right now?

Now swipe.

We are storytelling machines. Our minds take incomplete information and build an entire story around it. That served us well when all we had to base our stories on was our own experience. Now we have photos, AI slop videos, incomplete data and algorithms, and our minds will happily build stories out of the noise.

The same thing happens with data.

Slide 5 shows two real data points. The official poverty rate fell from 13.5% to 10.2% in ten years, and median household income rose. What story does your mind create about America?

Slide 6 adds a third, from our Single Income Stress Test. In the typical area, the median wage went from 139% of the local poverty threshold to 125%.

The problem is not the data. It is the story we create around it. So we need to stay vigilant and keep looking for more data to build a more complete story.

Today's post has our five takeaways: https://www.data4thepeople.com/p/five-takeaways-single-income
```

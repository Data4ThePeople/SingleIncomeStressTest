# Hero image prompts: The Single Income Stress Test: five takeaways

Target: 1680x1080 landscape. Generate wide (16:9 or 3:2); the crop is done with
`~/.claude/d4tp-process/hero fit`.

Rules for every prompt: no words, letters, numbers or logos anywhere in the
image; no recognizable faces; no flags or political symbols; leave calm, empty
space on one side so the crop has room.
